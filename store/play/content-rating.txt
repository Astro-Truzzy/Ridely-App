Ridely — Content rating (IARC questionnaire)
Play Console → App content → Content ratings. Complete the IARC form with these answers, then use whatever rating IARC returns. Do not guess a PEGI/ESRB label yourself.

App type
- Category: Utility / Productivity / Other  (Maps & Navigation is fine if offered)
- Not a game

Typical answers for Ridely as it works today
- Violence: No
- Sexuality / nudity: No
- Language: No (in-app chat is unmoderated user-to-user — if the form asks about user-generated content, say YES to users communicating)
- Controlled substances: No
- Gambling: No
- User interaction: YES — users can chat and voice-call each other (customer ↔ rider)
- Shares location: YES — live tracking during a delivery
- Digital purchases: YES if customers pay in-app via Paystack; this is a real-world service payment, not Google Play Billing. Answer the "unrestricted internet" / "users can purchase" questions honestly. You are NOT using Play Billing for deliveries.
- Age restriction: Riders must be 18+. The customer app is used by adults sending packages. Target age group: 18 and over is the safe choice.
- Appeals to children: NO. Do not include Ridely in Designed for Families.

Expected outcome
Utility apps with chat + location sharing often land around PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone or Teen. Submit the questionnaire and accept the official rating.
