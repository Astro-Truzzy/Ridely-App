This policy explains how [Legal entity name] (“Ridely”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Ridely mobile apps and related services. Contact: privacy@ridely.com. Address: [Registered business address].

1. Information we collect

You provide: name, email, phone number, profile photo, saved addresses, delivery details, payment and payout details (including rider bank account information), vehicle and verification documents for riders, and messages you send in chat or to support.

We collect automatically: precise and approximate location (to match riders, show live tracking, and complete deliveries), device information, app usage, crash diagnostics, and identifiers needed for login and push notifications.

Voice calls between a customer and a rider use the microphone during the call. We do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account
  • Book, match, track, and complete deliveries
  • Process payments (Paystack) and rider payouts
  • Enable in-app chat and voice calls for an active delivery
  • Send service notifications
  • Prevent fraud and keep the platform safe
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the product
  • Comply with law

3. When we share information

  • The other party on a delivery — name, pickup/drop-off details, live location during the trip, and chat or call content for that job
  • Paystack — to charge customers and pay riders
  • Maps and places providers — to geocode addresses and show maps
  • Infrastructure and diagnostics — hosting, authentication, and crash reporting
  • Authorities — when required by law

4. Location

Location is required for core Ridely features. Customers share pickup and drop-off points. Riders share location while online or on a job, including in the background, so we can offer nearby jobs and show live tracking. You can disable location in system settings; some features will stop working.

5. Retention and security

We keep account and delivery records for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal, tax, and dispute reasons. We use HTTPS and industry-standard safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Nigeria’s NDPR), you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your data. Email privacy@ridely.com or use Support in the app. You may also close your account through Support.

7. Children

Ridely is not directed at children. Riders must be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and in the app and change the date above.
