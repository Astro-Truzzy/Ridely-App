Ridely is the simple way to send a package across town — and a flexible way to earn as a rider.

Book a delivery in seconds. A nearby rider picks up your package, you watch it move on a live map, and a one-time handoff code keeps pickup and drop-off safe. Pay by card through Paystack or with cash when the rider arrives.

CUSTOMERS
• Book a delivery now, or schedule one for later
• Live tracking with ETA so you always know where your package is
• Safe handoff codes at pickup and drop-off
• Chat or voice-call your rider in the app
• Pay by card (Paystack) or cash on delivery
• Save favorite addresses and reorder in a tap
• Share a tracking link with the person receiving the package

RIDERS
• Go online and accept nearby jobs when it suits you
• See distance, payout, and route before you accept
• In-app navigation, chat, and voice calls with the customer
• Track earnings and request payouts to your Nigerian bank account
• Heatmap to find busier areas
• Verification and vehicle details so customers can trust the ride

Built for Nigerian cities, with prices in naira and payouts through Paystack.

Download Ridely and send your next package the simple way — or hop on as a rider and start earning.
